These are pattern-based rules for your firewall or proxy server. Wildcard domains use dynamic subdomains that cannot be tested directly. Share this list with your IT/security team.
📡 Subscription-Based Routing

Enterprise admins can use firewall rules to selectively allow or block Copilot by subscription plan. This works by routing traffic through plan-specific subdomains:

  • *.individual.githubcopilot.com — Free / Pro / Pro+ / Max (Student mapping is not documented)
  • *.business.githubcopilot.com — Business plan users
  • *.enterprise.githubcopilot.com — Enterprise plan users

Important: Do NOT add *.githubcopilot.com to your allowlist when using routing — it bypasses all plan restrictions. Below, Allow ✅ = your selected plan, Block 🚫 = plans to block or omit.
